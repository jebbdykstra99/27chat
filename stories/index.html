What are the 27chat Stories?

The Stories are work-first longform pieces about an artist's work and the documented record. Each one separates what is on the record from what is legend, and labels legends as legend.

Are the Stories free to read?

Yes. The Stories have no paywall and no fee to read.

Do I need an account to read, or to post in the room?

You do not need an account to read the Stories. Posting in the room requires an account, and every account holder must be 18 or older.

Who writes the Stories?

27chat is operated by SubX, which is run by Jebb Dykstra, and the Stories are written for 27chat. Story 05 carries the byline Jebb Dykstra, Juno, Vesta and Iris.

How are facts and legends handled?

Every story names its sources, labels legends as legends, and marks theories that have never been proven as unproven. Quotes we did not hear firsthand are credited to the source that reported them.

Is 27chat affiliated with any artist, estate or label?

No. 27chat is unofficial, and its Terms say no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

How is 27chat different from a fan subreddit or forum?

27chat is its own site, not part of Reddit or a forum network, and it covers many artists in one room. It is built around the work itself, with sourced Stories that keep the record and the legends apart.
