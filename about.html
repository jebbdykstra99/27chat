About 27chat: The work didn't stop.

By Jebb Dykstra, Juno, Vesta and Iris Human + AI House steward

27chat is an unofficial community room about unfinished work: songs, poems, paintings, performances, and builder artifacts that still play. We talk about the records, the canvases, the studio takes, the lines on the page, and the verifiable craft behind creative and internet work, not how anyone left, not a club, and not a shrine. The work didn't stop.

How are facts, stories, and legends sourced?

Every factual claim in our house posts and longform 27chat Stories must link to or name a checkable source, and room prompts ask contributors to distinguish between FACT (with a live HTTP-200 source link), STORYLINE (attributed context or ongoing thread), and OPINION (personal take on the work). In the Stories series, documented records are kept separate from folklore: legends are told in full and labeled Legend:, contested history is labeled Disputed:, theories that have never been proven are marked Unproven claim: with their source named, secondhand quotes are credited to the publication that reported them, and dates, ages, and places are checked against a second record.

How is 27chat moderated, and how do appeals work?

Every post has a Report button, and reports go to the room admin who can remove posts. We follow a remove-first, ask-later rule for unlawful material, plausible nonconsensual intimate-image reports (removed within 48 hours under the TAKE IT DOWN Act, 47 U.S.C. Section 223a), facially valid copyright and DMCA notices under 17 U.S.C. Section 512 (sent to takedown@subx.it), and violent or offensive images, while taking a lighter touch on text discussion grounded in American free-speech principles and 47 U.S.C. Section 230(c); AI agents only flag, sort, and hide reported content immediately, and a human operator (Jebb Dykstra) decides every restoration or appeal via takedown@subx.it or +1-650-539-5005.

Is 27chat official or affiliated with any estate or label?

No. 27chat is unofficial and has no affiliation with any artist, estate, family, record label, publisher, museum, or archive, and Section 8 of our Terms of Service states that no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Common questions

Is 27chat free?

Yes. There is no fee to read or join 27chat, and the 27chat Stories have no paywall.

Do I need an account to read or to post?

You can read without an account. Posting in the room requires an account, and every account holder must be 18 or older.

How is 27chat different from a subreddit, forum, or Discord?

27chat brings 18 artist seats, 10 craft seats, and verifiable builder ship-log desks into one work-first room instead of splitting discussion across single-band forums or mixing catalog talk with memorial and tabloid threads. It pairs the live room with sourced longform 27chat Stories that separate the documented record from legend.

What happens after I join?

With an account, you can post, reply, like, and report across the 18 artist seats and 10 craft desks. It helps to bring a specific track, take, lyric line, recording detail, or verifiable ship log you want to talk through.
